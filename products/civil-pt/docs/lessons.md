# Lessons — Medições PT

Written with `python3 factory/scripts/factory.py lesson` (keep the format): one line per lesson,
`- YYYY-MM-DD · <phase> · <kind> · <lesson>`. Kinds: mistake (what went wrong, and the fix), win
(what worked and should be repeated), method (a reusable technique), trend (a market, tech or
legal change, with its source), preference (what the founder wants). The weekly improvement cycle
(`/melhorar`) folds them into `factory/LEARNINGS.md` and `factory/knowledge/`.

- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · G2, Capterra and Software Advice return HTTP 403 to WebFetch, so review mining stalls below 30 items: plan for Trustpilot, Software Finder and search-result excerpts, and mark review quotes taken from excerpts as secondary extraction.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Vendor pricing pages (e.g. Kreo, Bluebeam) fetch cleanly and state currency and VAT, while competitor comparison pages and aggregators carry stale or conflicting prices: take the number from the vendor's own page and cite aggregators only as a cross-check.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · A quick locale-gap test for a localized clone is to read each incumbent's own language list and export-format list (e.g. native exchange formats) on its pricing or feature page, which gives a sourced fact instead of an inference from search absence.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · When curl cannot reach ine.pt, WebFetch can call the INE indicator API (json_indicador/pindica.jsp?op=2&varcd=<code>&Dim1=S7A<year>&Dim2=PT&Dim3=<CAE subclass>&Dim4=T,11,21,22,31,41) and pindicaMeta.jsp (dimension codes sit near the end of the file, use offset) to get enterprise counts by CAE and size band for bottom-up sizing.
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · A search summary treated a Portuguese public-procurement article (CCP art. 61) as in force while the consolidated 2017 text marks it revoked and moves the errors-and-omissions duty to art. 50: read legal articles in the primary consolidated text (download the PDF and run pdftotext -raw) instead of trusting search summaries.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Google Suggest with hl=pt-PT gl=PT is heavily contaminated by Brazilian Portuguese queries; count only terms that are specifically European Portuguese and treat zero-suggestion solution phrases as a demand signal rather than counting raw suggestion totals.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · When WebFetch cannot read a compressed PDF, the harness saves the binary under tool-results: copy it to the scratch dir and run pdftotext -layout to get the tables (worked for an AICCOPN sector barometer).
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · WebSearch is US-only and returns Brazilian pt-BR pages for Portuguese queries, so a Portugal SERP or community size cannot be inspected with it; mark SEO difficulty as low-confidence and plan a human or authenticated check, and note that WebFetch reads forumdacasa.com threads but gets 403 on G2, Workana and idealista and 503 on gee.gov.pt.

(source: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.15032)
