# Lessons — Medições PT

Written with `python3 factory/scripts/factory.py lesson` (keep the format): one line per lesson,
`- YYYY-MM-DD · <phase> · <kind> · <lesson>`. Kinds: mistake (what went wrong, and the fix), win
(what worked and should be repeated), method (a reusable technique), trend (a market, tech or
legal change, with its source), preference (what the founder wants). The weekly improvement cycle
(`/melhorar`) folds them into `factory/LEARNINGS.md` and `factory/knowledge/`.

- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · G2, Capterra and Software Advice return HTTP 403 to WebFetch, so review mining stalls below 30 items: plan for Trustpilot, Software Finder and search-result excerpts, and mark review quotes taken from excerpts as secondary extraction.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Vendor pricing pages (e.g. Kreo, Bluebeam) fetch cleanly and state currency and VAT, while competitor comparison pages and aggregators carry stale or conflicting prices: take the number from the vendor's own page and cite aggregators only as a cross-check.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · A quick locale-gap test for a localized clone is to read each incumbent's own language list and export-format list (e.g. native exchange formats) on its pricing or feature page, which gives a sourced fact instead of an inference from search absence.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · When curl cannot reach ine.pt, WebFetch can call the INE indicator API (json_indicador/pindica.jsp?op=2&varcd=<code>&Dim1=S7A<year>&Dim2=PT&Dim3=<CAE subclass>&Dim4=T,11,21,22,31,41) and pindicaMeta.jsp (dimension codes sit near the end of the file, use offset) to get enterprise counts by CAE and size band for bottom-up sizing.
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · A search summary treated a Portuguese public-procurement article (CCP art. 61) as in force while the consolidated 2017 text marks it revoked and moves the errors-and-omissions duty to art. 50: read legal articles in the primary consolidated text (download the PDF and run pdftotext -raw) instead of trusting search summaries.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Google Suggest with hl=pt-PT gl=PT is heavily contaminated by Brazilian Portuguese queries; count only terms that are specifically European Portuguese and treat zero-suggestion solution phrases as a demand signal rather than counting raw suggestion totals.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · When WebFetch cannot read a compressed PDF, the harness saves the binary under tool-results: copy it to the scratch dir and run pdftotext -layout to get the tables (worked for an AICCOPN sector barometer).
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · WebSearch is US-only and returns Brazilian pt-BR pages for Portuguese queries, so a Portugal SERP or community size cannot be inspected with it; mark SEO difficulty as low-confidence and plan a human or authenticated check, and note that WebFetch reads forumdacasa.com threads but gets 403 on G2, Workana and idealista and 503 on gee.gov.pt.
- 2026-11-26 · research · trend · Portuguese public-works tenders fell 39% in value (3,662 M EUR) and about 29% in number to June 2026 after a 2025 peak of 10,041 M EUR, which shrinks the near-term volume of bids for construction-estimating tools. (source: https://www.aiccopn.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/202607-BarometroAICCOPN-ObrasPublicas.pdf)
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · For AI measurement or extraction products, prove the deterministic path first with a 10-line local spike on a synthetic document (vector PDF geometry vs rasterised copy) and compute per-use model cost from the provider's official image-token formula, because general vision models score only about half of takeoff lines within ±25% on published benchmarks. (source: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.15032)
- 2026-11-26 · research · trend · Anthropic's first-party API offers inference_geo only as 'us' or 'global' and a US-only workspace geo (checked 2026-11-26); EU-only inference requires AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex, so 'data in the EU' claims for AI products need careful wording. (source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/data-residency)
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · EUR-Lex and some DRE pages return empty content through WebFetch and some regulator pages loop redirects, so primary legal texts could not be read; use the register in 07-legal.md plus labelled secondary sources and list the unread article in the gaps instead of quoting it.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · A synthesis can tell whether closing evidence gaps could change a verdict by recomputing the score with every low-confidence criterion at its cap of 3 and medium at 4: here that ceiling was 3.25, so the idea could reach PIVOT but never GO without new class A/B evidence.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Parallel research tracks can disagree on a legal deadline or universe size because one cites secondary summaries of an older statute and another reads the consolidated primary text and INE counts; list each contradiction in the synthesis and let the primary, newer, higher-class source win.
- 2026-11-26 · research · win · Bottom-up sizing from INE enterprise counts by CAE and size band, multiplied by competitor price points, showed a Portugal-only B2B niche has a base 12-month SOM near 17 k EUR before any channel work, which is the quickest way to flag that a small-country niche tool cannot sustain a solo founder.
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · A headline market trend (a -39% fall in public tenders) was cited against a niche segment without decomposing it by category in the same source table, where about 80% of the fall came from a different segment (buildings) and the niche categories were flat in value: break any headline trend down by segment, and check whether the latest year is a peak, before using it as evidence.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · For a list-based B2B niche (under ~2,000 identifiable buyers) a zero result in search-suggest tests is the expected null and cannot distinguish absent demand from demand sufficient for 15-50 customers, so score demand on list size and direct buyer conversations or paid-pilot intent and mark the search-volume anchors as not applicable.
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · A pessimistic check that only lowers low-confidence scores can never flag a KILL as fragile: when most criteria are low-confidence, also compute the optimistic mirror and the effect of +/-1 on the two criteria nearest the threshold, and state the verdict as a score band (here 2.55-3.00 around the 2.80 line) instead of a single number.
- 2026-11-26 · research · mistake · Search-autocomplete and search-volume anchors measure nothing in list-based B2B niches (a few thousand nameable buyers), so scoring zero suggestions as negative demand evidence is a rubric misuse: label the criterion 'not measured' and make a list-proof test (interviews or paid pilots) the deciding experiment.
- 2026-11-26 · research · method · Before citing a headline statistic from a trade-association bulletin, download the PDF and decompose it by segment (pdftotext -layout): a -39% fall in public tenders was 81% one sub-sector, while the buyer's segment was +2.7% in value and -31% in count.
- 2026-11-26 · research · trend · Spanish-language AI takeoff and estimating tools with PDF reading and BC3 export are priced at about EUR 10-30 per month (vendor-stated, 2026-11), a price floor that Iberian expansion of a Portuguese AI takeoff product would face. (source: https://www.buildgets.com/comparativa-software-presupuestos-obra.php)
