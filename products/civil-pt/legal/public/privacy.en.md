## 6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Your files are stored in the EU (Frankfurt), but that does not mean no data leaves the EEA: some of our providers are US or Singapore companies and may access data for technical support and operations, and e-mail sending (Resend) is processed in the United States. In those cases we rely on a European Commission adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified US companies) or on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Commission, together with additional safeguards where necessary. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards at [{{contact.privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{contact.privacyEmail}}).

## 7. How long we keep data

Retention periods are in section 2. In short, for files and the account:

- **Deleting a project:** the project's files and measurements are removed from storage within minutes.
- **Closing the account** (by you, or because the contract ends): for 30 days you can export your data; then we delete the account and all content. If you ask for immediate deletion, we do it without waiting, within one month at most.
- **Backups:** the database has daily backups that are replaced within 7 days; deleted data disappears from the backups in that period. PDF files have no backup.
- **Invoices:** the law requires us to keep invoices and accounting records for 10 years. These records are not deleted with the account.

When the periods end we delete the data or anonymise it irreversibly, unless the law requires longer storage or we need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with measures appropriate to the risk, including: encrypted connections (HTTPS); encryption at rest by our hosting providers; isolation between accounts enforced in the database, so one company never sees another's projects; private file storage with temporary access links; sign-in with a one-time code instead of a password, with a lock after failed attempts; keys and secrets kept out of the code; separation between test and production environments; logs without e-mail or project names; database backups; and careful selection of providers. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will inform you without undue delay and notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours whenever the law requires it.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: **access** your data; **rectification**; **erasure**; **restriction** of processing; **portability** (receive the data in a structured, commonly used format); **object** to processing based on legitimate interests (including business contacts) and to direct marketing; **withdraw consent**; and not to be subject to certain decisions based solely on automated processing.

To exercise a right, write to [{{contact.privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{contact.privacyEmail}}). You can delete your projects at any time in the application; to export or delete the whole account, use the same contact (or the account export and deletion options, when they exist). We may ask for information to confirm your identity. We reply without undue delay and within one month; if the request is complex or there are many requests we may extend this by two further months and will explain why within the first month. Exercising your rights is free of charge, unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

If a request concerns third parties' personal data contained in your drawings, we pass it on to your company, which is responsible for that data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Ours is the **{{legal.supervisoryAuthority}}** ([{{legal.supervisoryAuthorityUrl}}]({{legal.supervisoryAuthorityUrl}})); you can also complain to the authority of the EU country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place.
