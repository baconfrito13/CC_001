# Data Processing Agreement (Article 28 GDPR)

**Version:** 1 · **Date:** 2026-11-28

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") forms part of the agreement (the "**Agreement**", i.e. the Terms and Conditions) between the entity that accepts the Terms and Conditions of {{site.name}} (the "**Customer**", the controller) and **{{company.legalName}}**, {{company.address}}, {{company.country}}, tax number {{company.taxId}} (the "**Provider**", the processor), for the use of {{site.name}} (the "**Service**"). It applies where the Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Customer under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("**GDPR**") and Lei n.º 58/2019. If the Agreement and this DPA conflict on data protection, this DPA prevails.

## 1. Definitions

Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach", "supervisory authority" have the meaning in the GDPR. "**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data contained in the files, projects and results that the Customer uploads to or creates in the Service and that the Provider processes on the Customer's behalf. "**Sub-processor**" means a processor engaged by the Provider to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and instructions

2.1 The Customer is the controller and the Provider is the processor of Customer Personal Data. The Provider acts as an independent controller only for its own account, billing, security and product-metrics data, as described in its Privacy Policy.
2.2 The Provider processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are: the Agreement, this DPA, and the Customer's use and configuration of the Service. Any further instruction must be agreed in writing; the Provider may charge reasonable costs if it goes beyond the Service.
2.3 The Provider informs the Customer immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection law, and may suspend the instruction until it is confirmed or changed.
2.4 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, providing notices to data subjects and ensuring that its instructions comply with law, including its right to upload the drawings (project owner or designer authorisation, tender confidentiality).

## 3. Details of the processing

The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are set out in **Annex I**.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Provider ensures that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and process the data only as needed for the Service. The Provider does not read the Customer's files except with the Customer's authorisation for support, as needed to keep the Service secure and working, or where the law requires.

## 5. Security

The Provider implements technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, taking into account Article 32 GDPR, as described in **Annex II**. The Provider may update the measures provided that the overall level of security is not reduced.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for the Provider to use the Sub-processors listed in **Annex III**.
6.2 The Provider informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement at least **30 days** in advance (by e-mail to the Customer's account contact or by updating the published list with notification). The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period; the parties will discuss in good faith and, if unresolved, the Customer may terminate the affected Service without penalty and receive a pro rata refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.
6.3 The Provider imposes on each Sub-processor, by contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains liable to the Customer for the Sub-processor's performance.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Provider assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to data subject requests (Chapter III GDPR). If a data subject contacts the Provider directly about Customer Personal Data, the Provider forwards the request to the Customer without undue delay and does not respond except to redirect the person, unless the law requires otherwise.
7.2 The Provider assists the Customer with Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the information available to it. Reasonable costs for assistance beyond standard Service functionality may be charged.

## 8. Personal data breaches

The Provider notifies the Customer without undue delay and in any event within **48 hours** after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice describes, as far as known: the nature of the breach, categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. The Provider supplements the information as it becomes available and documents the breach. The Customer is responsible for notifying the supervisory authority and data subjects where required.

## 9. International transfers

9.1 The Provider stores and processes Customer Personal Data in the EU/EEA (Frankfurt, Germany) and only transfers it outside the EEA, including by remote access for technical support and operations, in accordance with Chapter V GDPR: to recipients covered by an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients), or under the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 with additional safeguards where needed.
9.2 Where the Customer is established outside the EEA, the Provider's processing remains subject to the GDPR as a processor established in the Union. The return or export of Customer Personal Data to the Customer takes place on the Customer's instruction and responsibility; if a Chapter V transfer tool is required, the parties agree one in good faith.

## 10. Return and deletion

At the end of the Service, or on the Customer's request, the Provider makes Customer Personal Data available for export for **30 days** and then deletes it within a further **30 days**, in practice promptly afterwards (database backups are replaced within 7 days; PDF files have no backup). The Customer may delete projects at any time in the Service. The Provider confirms deletion in writing on request, unless Union or Member State law requires storage.

## 11. Information and audits

The Provider makes available to the Customer the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates, subject to: reasonable written notice of at least 30 days; no more than once per 12 months unless a breach or authority request justifies more; confidentiality undertakings; no access to other customers' data; minimal disruption; and costs borne by the Customer. The Provider may satisfy the request in whole or in part by providing recent third-party audit reports or certifications of its Sub-processors or a completed security questionnaire.

## 12. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Agreement, to the extent the law allows. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law or the rights of data subjects under Article 82 GDPR.

## Annex II — Technical and organisational measures

*Measures required by the product architecture (docs/04-architecture.md, SR-01 to SR-14), to be confirmed by the QA and security review before go-live.*

- **Access control:** account isolation enforced in the database (row-level security on every table, tested account A cannot read account B); sign-in by one-time code with a lock after repeated failures; service keys held only in server-side code; no administration interface, administrative actions limited to the founder through service scripts that write an audit log.
- **Encryption:** TLS in transit; encryption at rest by the hosting and database providers.
- **Separation:** private storage buckets accessed through short-lived (maximum 10 minutes) signed links; separate test and production environments; production data never used in tests.
- **Logging and monitoring:** application logs without e-mail, project or file names (kept up to 30 days); error monitoring without personal data; availability monitoring.
- **Availability and resilience:** daily database backups (7 days); processing results can be regenerated; PDF files are not backed up (the Customer keeps the originals).
- **Vulnerability management:** exact dependency versions and lockfile; automated dependency updates; dependency audit in the build pipeline; upload limits (50 MB, 40 pages) and parser hardening against malicious PDFs.
- **Incident response:** documented procedure; notice to the Customer within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
- **Personnel:** confidentiality undertakings; the Provider is a sole-founder business and access to production is limited to the founder.
- **Vendor management:** data processing agreements with Sub-processors; due-diligence criteria in the Provider's sub-processor register.
- **Data minimisation and deletion:** the Service does not extract text from the drawings; a project can be deleted at any time (files removed within minutes); account deletion on request within 30 days.
