# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** {{legal.effectiveDate}} · **Last updated:** {{legal.lastUpdated}}

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) {{site.name}} uses on {{site.url}}, and how you control them. It applies Article 5 of Law no. 41/2004 (ePrivacy) and the GDPR.

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by the websites you visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage, serve the same purposes. Some are essential for a site to work; others require your consent.

## 2. What we use

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Consent needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `consent` (cookie and local storage item) | {{site.domain}} | Remembers your cookie choice | 180 days | No (strictly necessary) |
| Analytics identifiers (if an analytics tool is enabled) | Our analytics provider | Measure which pages are visited so we can improve the Site | Up to 12 months | **Yes** — loaded only after you accept |
| Payment provider cookies | Payment provider or Merchant of Record | Process and secure your payment on their pages | Set by the provider | No for security and payment; see their policies |

Where we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool that sets no cookies and stores no personal data on your device, it may run without consent; we say so here when it applies.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 3. How to manage your choice

The first time you visit, a banner lets you **accept** or **reject** analytics with the same ease. Rejecting has no effect on how you use the Site. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of every page. Your choice is stored for 180 days.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; the Site keeps working, but you may be asked for your choice again.
