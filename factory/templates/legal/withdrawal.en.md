## 3. Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from the contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event no later than **14 days** from the day on which we are informed of your decision. We will use the same means of payment you used for the initial transaction, unless you expressly agree otherwise; in any event you will not incur any fee as a result of the reimbursement. After you withdraw you must stop using the digital content or service and must not make it available to third parties.

## 4. Services that start during the withdrawal period

If you asked for the service to begin before the 14 days have expired, you will pay us an amount proportionate to what has been provided until you informed us of your withdrawal, in relation to the full coverage of the contract. You owe nothing if we did not give you the information about this, or if you did not expressly ask us to start during the withdrawal period. If the contract is fully performed after your express request and your acknowledgement that you lose the right of withdrawal in that case, you can no longer withdraw.

If any of these conditions is missing, you keep your right to withdraw for 14 days and owe nothing for the content supplied.
