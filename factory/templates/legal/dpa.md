## 1. Definitions

Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach", "supervisory authority" have the meaning in the GDPR. "**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data processed by the Provider on the Customer's behalf through the Service. "**Sub-processor**" means a processor engaged by the Provider to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and instructions

2.1 The Customer is the controller and the Provider is the processor of Customer Personal Data. The Provider acts as an independent controller only for its own account, billing and security data, as described in its Privacy Policy.
2.2 The Provider processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are: the Agreement, this DPA, and the Customer's use and configuration of the Service. Any further instruction must be agreed in writing; the Provider may charge reasonable costs if it goes beyond the Service.
2.3 The Provider informs the Customer immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection law, and may suspend the instruction until it is confirmed or changed.
2.4 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, providing notices to data subjects and ensuring that its instructions comply with law.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Provider ensures that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and process the data only as needed for the Service.

## 5. Security

The Provider implements technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, taking into account Article 32 GDPR, as described in **Annex II**. The Provider may update the measures provided that the overall level of security is not reduced.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for the Provider to use the Sub-processors listed in **Annex III**.
6.2 The Provider informs the Customer of any intended addition or replacement at least **30 days** in advance (by e-mail to the Customer's account contact or by updating the published list with notification). The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period; the parties will discuss in good faith and, if unresolved, the Customer may terminate the affected Service without penalty and receive a pro rata refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.
6.3 The Provider imposes on each Sub-processor, by contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains liable to the Customer for the Sub-processor's performance.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Provider assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to data subject requests (Chapter III GDPR). If a data subject contacts the Provider directly about Customer Personal Data, the Provider forwards the request to the Customer without undue delay and does not respond except to redirect the person, unless the law requires otherwise.
7.2 The Provider assists the Customer with Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the information available to it. Reasonable costs for assistance beyond standard Service functionality may be charged.

## 8. Personal data breaches

The Provider notifies the Customer without undue delay and in any event within **48 hours** after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice describes, as far as known: the nature of the breach, categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. The Provider supplements the information as it becomes available and documents the breach. The Customer is responsible for notifying the supervisory authority and data subjects where required.

## 9. International transfers

9.1 The Provider processes Customer Personal Data in [the EU/EEA] and only transfers it outside the EEA in accordance with Chapter V GDPR: to recipients covered by an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients), or under the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 with additional safeguards where needed.
9.2 Where the Customer is established outside the EEA, the Provider's processing remains subject to the GDPR as a processor established in the Union. The return or export of Customer Personal Data to the Customer takes place on the Customer's instruction and responsibility; if a Chapter V transfer tool is required, the parties agree one in good faith [LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED before accepting a non-EEA customer with special requirements].

## 11. Information and audits

The Provider makes available to the Customer the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates, subject to: reasonable written notice of at least 30 days; no more than once per 12 months unless a breach or authority request justifies more; confidentiality undertakings; no access to other customers' data; minimal disruption; and costs borne by the Customer. The Provider may satisfy the request in whole or in part by providing recent third-party audit reports or certifications [e.g. ISO 27001 / SOC 2, if any] or a completed security questionnaire.
