## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage, software identifiers in apps and tracking pixels, work in a comparable way. In this policy we call all of them "cookies".

Cookies can be:

- **Session cookies**, which disappear when you close your browser, or **persistent cookies**, which stay for a set period.
- **First-party cookies**, set by us, or **third-party cookies**, set by another company whose service we use.

## 2. The rules and our approach

In Portugal and the European Union, storing information on your device, or accessing information already stored there, requires your **prior consent**, except where it is **strictly necessary** to provide a service you have expressly requested or to carry out a communication (Lei n.º 41/2004, article 5, which implements the ePrivacy Directive).

Where we need your consent:

- we ask before anything is stored or read;
- refusing is as easy as accepting, and no option is pre-selected;
- we do not make the service depend on accepting cookies that are not necessary for it;
- you can change your mind at any time.

When cookies involve personal data, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also applies; see our Privacy Policy.

## 3. Types of cookies

- **Strictly necessary:** needed to provide the service you asked for, for example keeping you signed in, protecting forms against forgery, balancing server load or remembering your cookie choice. They do not need your consent.
- **Preferences:** remember settings such as language or layout when they are not strictly necessary to deliver the service. They need your consent.
- **Statistics:** help us understand how the service is used. They need your consent when they store or read information on your device.
- **Marketing:** build profiles or show and measure advertising. They always need your consent.
- **Embedded content:** videos, maps or social posts supplied by other companies, which may set their own cookies. They need your consent.
